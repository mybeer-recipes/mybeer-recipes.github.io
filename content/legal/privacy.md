This policy explains how mybeer.recipes handles personal data when you use our apps for iOS, Android, macOS, Windows, Linux and the web, the MyBeer Ingredients supplier console, and the website at mybeer.recipes (together, the "Services").

It is written to meet the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, and US state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the CPRA.

## Who we are

mybeer.recipes is operated by Ross Merrigan, based in Switzerland ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy, except where we say we act for someone else (see [Breweries and suppliers](#breweries-and-suppliers)).

For anything about privacy or this policy, email **legal@mybeer.recipes**.

### Contact for the EU and the UK

Ross Merrigan handles every privacy request from people in the EU and the UK directly. Email **legal@mybeer.recipes**, and you'll get a reply from him, not a third party.

Because we are based outside the EU and the UK, the GDPR and the UK GDPR also require us to appoint a representative in each. We will name them here once they are appointed. Until then, and afterwards, you can always contact us directly.

## Personal data we collect

### Data you give us

- **Account details:** your email address, a password (stored only as a salted argon2id hash, never in readable form), and, if you add them, your display name, profile photo, short bio, phone number, country, language and time zone.
- **Sign-in and security settings:** passkeys and two-factor settings (authenticator app, email code or security key). If you sign in with Apple, Google, or your company's single sign-on, we receive your name and email from that provider.
- **Brewery and team details:** your brewery's name, locations, brewhouses and equipment, the people you invite (their email address) and the role each one holds.
- **Your brewing records:** recipes, batches, readings, notes, inventory, lots, shopping lists, water profiles and images you upload. These usually aren't personal data, but they are linked to your account.
- **Shared recipes:** if you publish a recipe to the shared library, other users see it with your public profile name and brewery, and how many times it was viewed, downloaded and rated.
- **Messages:** what you send us when you ask for help or give feedback.
- **Sign-up forms:** on this website, the email address and the other details you enter in the beta, newsletter, developer waitlist or supplier early-access forms (such as your name, whether you're a homebrewer or a brewery, your brewery's name and your devices).
- **Your choices:** whether you accepted the terms, confirmed your age, and which emails and notifications you want.

### Data collected when you use the Services

- **Device and app data:** app version, operating system, and the device's name or model. For push notifications we store a push token for each device.
- **Security logs:** sign-ins, failed attempts and changes to your security settings. We store a one-way hash of your IP address, not the address itself, together with your browser or app's user agent.
- **Crash reports:** in the mobile and desktop brewer apps, Firebase Crashlytics sends a report when the app crashes. A report includes the device model, operating system, app version, the code that failed and an anonymous installation ID.
- **Product usage:** the apps record which screens and features you use (for example, opening a recipe or viewing an ingredient), so we can see what works and what to improve. These events go to our own servers only, not to advertising or analytics companies.
- **Website analytics:** only if you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics 4 records the pages you visit, how you got here, and your approximate location, browser and device. Your IP address is shortened before it is stored, and Google's advertising features are turned off.

### Data from others

- **Your brewery or supplier company:** when someone invites you, we receive your email address and the role they gave you.
- **App stores:** when you subscribe, Apple or Google tells us (through RevenueCat) which plan you bought, when it renews and whether it is active. We never receive your card or bank details.
- **Connected devices and services:** if you connect a fermentation monitor or other brewing software, we receive the readings and data it sends for your brewery.

We do not collect special categories of personal data (such as health or religious data), and we ask you not to put any in your notes or messages.

## International transfers

We store your account and brewing data in the European Union. Switzerland, the EU and the UK each recognise the others as giving adequate protection.

Some providers above are in the United States, or may access data from there. For those transfers we rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, its UK Extension and the Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified. Otherwise we rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, with the UK addendum and the amendments the Swiss FDPIC requires. Email us for a copy of the relevant safeguards.

## How long we keep it

- **Your account and brewing data:** for as long as your account is open. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete it and its data, including mailing list records, within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep something longer.
- **Deleted items:** recipes, batches and other records you delete stay in a trash for up to 90 days, so they can be restored, and are then removed for good.
- **Device push tokens:** deleted after 60 days without use.
- **Email delivery events:** kept for 30 days.
- **Data exports:** a download link to your export expires after 7 days.
- **Security logs, crash reports and usage events:** kept only as long as needed for security, troubleshooting and analysis. Google Analytics keeps website data for at most 14 months.
- **Mailing lists:** until you unsubscribe, or we stop the list.
- **Subscription records:** as long as accounting and tax law requires, usually up to 10 years.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of these rights:

- **Access:** get a copy of the personal data we hold about you, and information about how we use it.
- **Correction:** fix data that is wrong or incomplete. You can change most of it yourself in the app.
- **Deletion:** ask us to delete your data.
- **Portability:** receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format, or have it sent to another provider where that is possible.
- **Restriction and objection:** ask us to limit how we use your data, or object to processing based on legitimate interests, including product usage analysis. You can always object to direct marketing.
- **Withdraw consent:** at any time, for anything based on consent, such as emails or analytics cookies. This doesn't affect what we did before.
- **Complain to a regulator:** in Switzerland, to the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC); in the EU, to the data protection authority where you live or work; in the UK, to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). We'd appreciate the chance to sort things out with you first.

To use any of these rights, email **legal@mybeer.recipes** from the email address on your account. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We reply within one month (extendable by two more months for complex requests, which we'll tell you about), and within 30 days under the Swiss FADP. We don't charge for requests unless they are clearly unfounded or excessive.

### US state privacy rights

If you live in California or another US state with a comprehensive privacy law (such as Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas or Oregon), you have the right to:

- know what personal information we collect, use and disclose, and get a copy of it;
- correct and delete it;
- opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, targeted advertising and profiling. We don't do any of these;
- limit the use of sensitive personal information. We only use it to provide the Services, such as your account password;
- not be treated differently for using these rights;
- appeal our decision on your request, by replying to it. If we refuse your appeal, you can contact your state's attorney general.

In the last 12 months we collected the categories of personal information described in [Personal data we collect](#personal-data-we-collect): identifiers (such as name, email and device IDs), customer records, commercial information (subscriptions), internet activity (usage and, with consent, website analytics), approximate location from website analytics, and inferences about which features you use. We collected them from you, your devices, your team and the services above, for the purposes in [How we use it and why](#how-we-use-it-and-why), and disclosed them only to the categories of recipients in [Who we share it with](#who-we-share-it-with).

You can send requests by email, or through an authorised agent with your signed permission. We treat a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser as a request to opt out, and this website then keeps analytics off.

## Cookies

This website uses one cookie of its own, `mybeer.recipes.privacy`. It remembers your choice in the cookie banner for 365 days, and is needed for the site to respect that choice.

Analytics cookies are set only if you select **Accept all**:

| Cookie | Set by | Purpose | Kept for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `_ga` | Google Analytics | Tells visits from the same browser apart | 2 years |
| `_ga_<id>` | Google Analytics | Keeps the state of the current visit | 2 years |

You can change your choice at any time with **Cookie settings** at the bottom of every page. Declining or withdrawing consent stops analytics from loading. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.

The web app stores your account session and an offline copy of your data in your browser's storage. This is needed for the app to work and is not used for tracking.

## Security

We protect personal data with encryption in transit (TLS), access controls, hashed passwords, hashed IP addresses in our logs, and passkeys and two-factor sign-in that you can turn on. Staff access to account data is limited and every staff action is recorded in an audit log. No system is completely secure, so if a breach puts your rights at risk, we will notify you and the regulators as the law requires.

## Breweries and suppliers

When a brewery or a supplier company invites people to its account, it decides what its members can see and do there. The brewery or company is responsible for having a lawful basis to invite its members and to store data about its own staff and customers in the Services.

**MyBeer Ingredients** is our console for suppliers of brewing ingredients. For supplier members we process their name, email address, role, sign-in and last-active time, and a log of the changes each member makes. Product, pack, lot and company information that a supplier publishes is visible to everyone using mybeer.recipes. Suppliers should avoid putting personal data into published product information. If the supplier uses single sign-on, we receive the member's name and email from the company's identity provider.

## Children

The Services are about brewing alcohol and are only for adults: you must be at least 18 and of legal drinking age where you live. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email legal@mybeer.recipes and we'll delete it.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Services or the law change. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change significantly affects how we use your data, we will tell you by email or in the app before it takes effect.
