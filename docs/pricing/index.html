Questions about billing

How am I billed?

Through the store you subscribe in: the App Store, Google Play or the Microsoft Store. Prices shown are in US dollars; your store shows the price in your currency, including any VAT.

Can I change tier later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade from Settings in the app. The store applies the change and adjusts what you pay.

How do I cancel?

Cancel any time in your store account. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for, and your recipes stay in your account.

Which tier should a homebrewer choose?

Nano covers home and very small scale brewing, under 200 L a batch.
