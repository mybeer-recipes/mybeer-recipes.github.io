Early access MyBeer Ingredients

Put your ingredients in brewers' recipes

MyBeer Ingredients is the console for maltsters, hop merchants, yeast labs and homebrew shops. Keep your catalogue, specifications, lots and stock in one place, and brewers on mybeer.recipes add your products to their recipes with your own figures. It runs in your web browser, with nothing to install.

Request early access What brewers see

  • Catalogue

    Products with their full specifications, packs and prices, photos and documents. Publish a product when it's ready and brewers can find it.

  • Lots and stock

    Every lot with its own analysis and best-before date, across your warehouses, so you see what is running low or nearing its date.

  • Supplier sheets

    Build data sheets, certificates of analysis and catalogues from your product data, and export them as PDF, Excel, CSV or BeerProto.

  • Barcodes and labels

    GS1 barcodes for every pack and lot, from EAN-13 and GS1-128 to DataMatrix and Digital Link QR codes. Print labels, or export SVG and EPS for artwork.

  • See how brewers use them

    How many recipes use each product, how often brewers add, view and download them, and which products need stock.

  • Members and roles

    Invite your team as owner, admin, editor, stock or viewer, so each person can change only what their role allows.

What brewers see

  • Your products in the mybeer.recipes ingredient catalogue, with your specifications and packs.
  • A button that copies your product into their own library, ready for a recipe.
  • A link to your shop from every product page.
  • The product and its lot, opened from the QR code on the pack.
