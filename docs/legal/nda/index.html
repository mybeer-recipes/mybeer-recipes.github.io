This agreement is between you and Ross Merrigan, based in Switzerland (“we”, “us”), who runs mybeer.recipes. It applies when you join a beta, preview or early-access programme for the mybeer.recipes apps, the web app or MyBeer Ingredients (the “Beta”).

You accept it when you accept a beta invitation or install a pre-release build. If you join for a brewery or supplier company, you accept it for that organisation, and you make sure everyone you invite to the Beta follows it too.

Our Terms of service and Privacy policy also apply to the Beta. If this agreement conflicts with the terms of service about the Beta, this agreement applies.

What the Beta is

The Beta gives you early access to versions of the Services that are still being built (“Pre-release software”), so you can try them and tell us what works. Pre-release software may be incomplete, contain errors, change often, or be withdrawn. We may end the Beta, or your place in it, at any time.

What is confidential

“Confidential information” means anything about the Beta that we haven’t made public, including:

  • unreleased features, designs, screens and their wording;
  • plans, prices, roadmaps and release dates we share with you;
  • pre-release builds, test accounts and access links;
  • anything we mark or describe as confidential.

It does not include information that:

  • is or becomes public without you breaking this agreement;
  • you already knew, without a duty of confidentiality, before we shared it;
  • you receive lawfully from someone else who is free to share it;
  • you develop independently, without using our confidential information.

What you agree to

  • Keep it private. Don’t share confidential information with anyone outside the Beta, including by posting screenshots, recordings, reviews or descriptions online. Protect it with at least reasonable care.
  • Use it only for testing. Use the Pre-release software and confidential information only to test the Beta and send us feedback.
  • Don’t share access. Don’t pass on builds, invite links or test accounts. Ask us to invite anyone else who should take part.
  • Don’t take it apart. Don’t copy, sell or reverse engineer the Pre-release software, except where the law allows it despite this restriction.
  • Tell us about leaks. If confidential information is disclosed by mistake, tell us straight away at legal@mybeer.recipes.

You may discuss confidential information with your own professional advisers, under a duty of confidentiality, or when the law or a court requires it. In that case, tell us first if the law allows.

You are always free to say that you are taking part in the mybeer.recipes beta. Anything we have released publicly, or said you may share, is no longer confidential.

Feedback

We’d love your feedback, bug reports and ideas. By sending them, you allow us to use them freely to improve our products, without any obligation or payment to you. We won’t publish your name with your feedback without asking.

Your data in the Beta

Recipes, batches and other data you create in the Beta stay in your account after launch, as the beta sign-up page says. Because pre-release software can have bugs, keep your own copies of anything important. We may need to reset test data in exceptional cases, and we will warn you in advance whenever we can.

We handle your personal data as our Privacy policy describes. Pre-release builds may collect more diagnostic data, such as crash reports and usage events, to help us find problems.

No warranty and no support commitment

The Pre-release software is provided “as is”, for testing. To the extent the law allows, we make no promises that it will work correctly, be available, or keep your data intact, and we have no duty to provide support or updates, although we’ll help where we can. We don’t promise to release any feature, or a commercial version of the Beta.

To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for loss arising from your use of the Pre-release software. Nothing in this agreement limits liability for intent or gross negligence, or any liability that the law does not allow to be limited.

How long it lasts

This agreement applies while you take part in the Beta. When you leave the Beta, or it ends, stop using pre-release builds that we ask you to remove, and delete any confidential materials we’ve sent you.

Your duty to keep information confidential continues for 2 years after your participation ends, or until we make the information public, whichever comes first.

Law and disputes

This agreement is governed by Swiss law. The courts of the canton of Vaud, Switzerland, have jurisdiction. If you are a consumer living in another country, you keep the protection of the mandatory laws of that country, and you may bring a claim in the courts where you live.

General

  • This agreement, with the terms of service and privacy policy, is the whole agreement between you and us about the Beta.
  • We may update this agreement, and will tell you about significant changes before they take effect. If you don’t agree, you can leave the Beta.
  • If part of this agreement can’t be enforced, the rest still applies.
  • If we don’t enforce a right straight away, we can still enforce it later.
  • This agreement is personal to you, and you may not transfer it. We may transfer it to a company that takes over the Services.

Contact

Questions about this agreement: legal@mybeer.recipes. Bugs and help during the Beta: support@mybeer.recipes.
